Angaraag Mahanta (born 24 November 1975), known by his stage name Papon, is an Indian playback singer, composer and songwriter from Assam. His music draws on Assamese folk traditions, Indian classical music, electronic production and contemporary popular music. He has recorded in Assamese, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Marathi, Urdu, Mising, Bodo, Tiwa, Punjabi, Odia and Kannada.[2][3]

He is the lead vocalist and founder of the folk-fusion band Papon and The East India Company. He is the son of Assamese musicians Khagen Mahanta and Archana Mahanta.

Career

Early career

Papon began his professional musical journey in Assam and released the Assamese album Junaki Raati in 2004. His first Hindi-film song was “Sajna Baware” (also styled “Sajana”) from Let’s Enjoy (2004). In a Reuters interview, he said that he sang, wrote and produced the track, which was later co-produced by MIDIval Punditz.[4][5]

He received wider recognition with “Jiyein Kyun” from Dum Maaro Dum (2011), followed by songs including “Kyon” from Barfi! (2012), “Moh Moh Ke Dhaage” from Dum Laga Ke Haisha (2015), “Humnava” from Hamari Adhuri Kahani (2015) and “Bulleya” from Sultan (2016). In 2018, he sang “Baba Bolta Hain Bas Ho Gaya” for Sanju.

Later work

His later projects included contributions to the soundtrack of Axone (2020), the music project Unacademy Unwind (2021), and songs associated with Metro... In Dino (2025). He also recorded songs for Tu Meri Poori Kahani (2025).[6][7][8][9]

In 2026, he launched Mehfilz with Papon, a contemporary ghazal project presented through intimate living-room performances and collaborations with lyricists including Irshad Kamil.[16][17]

Band

In 2007, Papon formed the electronic folk-fusion group Papon and The East India Company. The group combines Assamese folk and Indian classical influences with rock, blues, trip hop and live electronic music. It has performed at festivals in India and abroad.

Collaborations

Papon has collaborated with film composers, independent musicians and folk performers. His work includes collaborations with Pritam, Shantanu Moitra, MIDIval Punditz, Vishal–Shekhar, Anu Malik, Rabbi Shergill and Harshdeep Kaur. He has appeared on Coke Studio @ MTV and The Dewarists.

Philanthropy and social initiatives

Papon and Shweta Mishra Mahanta founded the Earthful Foundation in 2018. The organisation works in areas including waste management, sustainable livelihoods, health, nutrition and environmental awareness.[12]

In 2014, Papon and Vishal Dadlani spearheaded the #FORASSAM fundraising concert in Delhi for people affected by floods in Assam. He revived the initiative during the 2019 floods, combining fundraising with relief work by volunteers who were described in media reports as the “Papon Army”.[13][14]

Personal life and education

Papon was raised in a musical family and received early exposure to Assamese folk, devotional and classical traditions through his parents. He later studied in Delhi, attending Ramjas College and Motilal Nehru College. He lives in Mumbai with his wife, Shweta Mishra Mahanta, and their children.
