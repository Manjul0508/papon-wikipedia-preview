Papon has recorded film songs, regional-language songs, independent singles, albums and televised performances across multiple Indian languages. The tables below reconcile the current public song list with source-audited corrections and releases located through 8 August 2026.

Hindi film songs

“Sajna Baware” / “Sajana” from Let’s Enjoy (2004) is placed first because Papon directly described it as his first Bollywood number, which he sang, wrote and produced before MIDIval Punditz co-produced it.[1]
