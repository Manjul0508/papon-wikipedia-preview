## Court-order finding

The attached certified order dated 6 December 2019 says that the investigating officer did not find sufficient evidentiary material to file a charge sheet, described the case as arising from a mistake of fact, and that the final report was accepted and the proceeding closed.

A targeted search found extensive reputable reporting about the original 2018 complaint, but no strong independent news article specifically reporting the December 2019 closure. The Wikimedia Commons page hosts the order image but is not independent secondary reporting. For a real Wikipedia request, the order should be used only for its narrow wording, and an official eCourts record or independent article would be preferable.

## Public-catalogue limitation

The song page is substantially broader than the current article and includes public listings through 2026. It should not be described as a guaranteed record-label master catalogue because streaming services can contain duplicates, reissues, alternate versions and missing regional releases.
