Source-audited draft. This page separates strong article-ready entries from award leads that still need an official or stronger organiser source. This prevents weak database entries from being shown as established facts.

Papon has received and been nominated for awards for playback singing, Assamese cinema and web-original music. The 2016 award season for “Moh Moh Ke Dhaage” is the strongest cluster in the public record, while the audit also identified later Assamese and digital/web recognition including a 2024 Prag Cine Award win and a 2026 News18 Showsha Reel Awards nomination.
